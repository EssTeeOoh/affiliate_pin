## Buying guide

If you are building a setup from scratch, start with the items that affect daily comfort first. Keyboard, mouse, and desk surface upgrades usually deliver the most noticeable improvement. After that, add lighting and cable management to refine the overall look.

For a clean, practical setup:

- Choose the keyboard first if you type or game a lot
- Choose the mouse first if wireless simplicity matters most
- Choose the mouse pad if you want a unified desk surface
- Choose lighting if the room needs more depth
- Choose cable management if the desk still looks messy

## Final recommendation

The best value comes from choosing accessories that actually improve how the desk feels to use. A better keyboard and mouse improve interaction, the mouse pad improves the surface, lighting improves the atmosphere, and cable management ties the whole setup together.

If you want a balanced starting point, begin with the keyboard, mouse, and cable management tool, then add the RGB desk mat and lighting as the final polish.
