# Pinterest + Amazon Associates Setup Guide

## Niche

Focus: **Tech, Desk Setup & Productivity Finds** with gaming as a
subtopic.

Boards: - Gaming Desk Setup - Gaming Accessories - Budget Tech Gadgets -
PC Setup Ideas - Home Office Setup - Mobile Gaming Accessories

## Workflow

1.  Research 5 products (4.5★+ preferred, strong sales).
2.  Write ONE blog article.
3.  Add affiliate disclosure.
4.  Add Amazon affiliate buttons.
5.  Create 5 Pinterest Pins pointing to the article.
6.  Publish Pins over several days.

## Article Taxonomy

Every article should use:

- One primary category
- Up to two secondary categories

Suggested pattern:

- Primary: Gaming
- Secondary: Desk Setup, Cable Management and Organization

Keep the categories aligned with the site's main category pages so the article supports the broader catalog.
