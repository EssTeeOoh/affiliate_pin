# Content Publishing SOP

## Article Template

Title

Intro that explains who the guide is for and what problem it solves.

Repeat for each of 5 products:
- Product
- Why it's useful
- Pros
- Cons
- Best for
- Amazon button

Conclusion + FAQ or final recommendation.

## Article Categories

Use one primary category and up to two secondary categories for every article.

- Primary category: the main SEO and editorial topic
- Secondary categories: supporting topics, limited to a maximum of two
- Keep the labels consistent with the site categories

Example:

- Primary category: Gaming
- Secondary categories: Desk Setup, Cable Management and Organization

## Pinterest Pin

Title: Top 5 ...

Description: Use keywords naturally. Mention the benefit and invite
readers to see the full guide.

Pin links to the blog article.
